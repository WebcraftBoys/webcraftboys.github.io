How we work

A clear process means better work.

You shouldn't have to wonder what happens next. We break every project into clear stages so you always know where we are, what we're working on and what we need from you.

The workflow

Four stages. One clear direction.

Every project is different, but the foundation stays consistent. We move from understanding the problem to building the right solution.

01
Discover

Understand before we build.

Before touching the design, we learn about your business, your customers and what the website actually needs to achieve.

Business goals Target audience Website requirements Content & direction
YOU PROVIDE Context
02
Design

Turn the direction into a visual system.

We create the structure, visual language and user experience of your website. The goal is not simply to make it look good, but to make it make sense.

Page structure Layout & hierarchy Typography Visual direction
YOU REVIEW Direction
03
Build

Make the design work in the real world.

Once the direction is approved, we turn the design into a responsive, functional website that works across modern devices.

Front-end development Responsive layouts Interactions Forms & functionality
WE HANDLE Development
04
Launch

Get your website ready for real visitors.

Before launch, we check the important details, test the experience and prepare the website for deployment.

Device testing Final checks Performance review Deployment & handover
FINAL RESULT Live website
Your part

What we need from you.

You don't need technical knowledge to work with us. Your most important job is simply helping us understand your business.

01

Tell us about your business.

What you do, who you serve and what makes your business different.

02

Share your content.

Provide the information, images, branding and details that should appear on the site.

03

Give honest feedback.

Tell us what feels right, what doesn't and what you want visitors to understand.
