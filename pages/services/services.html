FAQs

Before we get
started.

A few common questions about planning and building a website.

What type of website is right for my business?

It depends on your goals, audience and content. A small service business may need a focused set of pages for services, about, FAQs and enquiries. A campaign may need a single landing page. We'll help determine a sensible scope before building.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Responsive design is part of the build. We plan and check layouts for smaller screens as well as desktop, including readable content, navigation and touch-friendly interactions.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of starting over?

Yes. We can review what is already working, identify usability or visual issues and decide whether to refresh the existing structure or rebuild parts of it. The approach depends on your current site and goals.

Will the website be SEO-friendly?

We build with SEO fundamentals in mind, including meaningful page titles, descriptions, heading structure, mobile responsiveness, internal links and crawlable content. SEO foundations help search engines understand your pages, but rankings or traffic cannot be guaranteed.

Can I request changes after the website launches?

Yes. Changes and further improvements can be planned after launch. Depending on what you need, we can discuss updates, maintenance or a separate improvement project.

How do I get started with Webcraft?

Send us a short description of your business, what the website should do and any examples or ideas you have. We can then discuss the project scope and next steps.
