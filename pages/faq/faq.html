Questions, answered

Let's make the process clear.

Here are the things clients usually want to know before starting a website project with Webcraft.

What kind of websites do you build?

We build business websites, landing pages, portfolio sites, restaurant and local-business websites, e-commerce experiences and redesigns. The scope is shaped around what the website needs to achieve.

How long does a website take?

A focused website can often move from planning to launch in a few weeks. Larger projects take longer depending on content, number of pages, integrations and feedback cycles.

Do you provide the design and development?

Yes. We handle the visual direction and the front-end build as one process, which keeps the final website consistent and avoids handoff gaps.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We can review an existing site, identify usability and visual issues, and improve the structure, responsiveness, content hierarchy and calls to action.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Responsive behaviour is part of the build rather than an afterthought. We design layouts to remain useful and polished across phones, tablets and desktops.

Do you maintain websites after launch?

We can help with ongoing updates, improvements, content changes and general website maintenance depending on the needs of the project.

How do I start?

Send us a project enquiry with a little context about your business and what you need. We'll review it and come back with the right next step.
